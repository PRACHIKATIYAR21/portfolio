A Little About Me

Hi, I'm Prachi 👋 An aspiring AI/ML professional.

I am a Computer Science Engineering student at Prestige Institute of Engineering Management & Research, Indore. I am passionate about exploring Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science.

I enjoy working with data — from cleaning and preprocessing datasets to discovering patterns and building machine learning models. Python and Jupyter Notebook are my primary tools for learning and experimentation.

My hands-on work includes a Heart Disease Prediction web application using Streamlit and an ongoing Smart Air Quality Monitoring and Prediction project that combines machine learning with a hardware prototype.

As the President of my college's AI Club, I also enjoy contributing to AI-focused activities and collaborating with fellow students. My goal is to keep learning, build practical solutions, and grow in the field of AI and Machine Learning.
